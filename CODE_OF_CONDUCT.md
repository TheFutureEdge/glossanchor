# Code of Conduct

We are committed to a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free community.
Participants must communicate constructively, respect differing viewpoints,
accept responsibility for mistakes, and focus feedback on the work rather than
the person.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized conduct, publication of private
information, and sustained disruption are unacceptable. Maintainers may edit,
remove, or reject contributions and may temporarily or permanently restrict
participation when necessary to protect the community.

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository maintainers. Reports will
be handled as confidentially and promptly as practical.
